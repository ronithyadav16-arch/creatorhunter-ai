FAQ

Before you create.

Does it generate real video footage right now?

Not in this free static version. It creates an editable storyboard, script, caption ideas, and exportable project brief. Real AI video rendering requires a compatible model or service.

Do I need an API key or account?

No. The demo studio works in your browser and does not require a paid API or sign-in.

Where are my projects saved?

Projects and brand-kit preferences are stored in this browser using local storage. They do not automatically sync between devices.

Can I use this to plan videos for my editing workflow?

Yes. Use the storyboard and script as a starting point for editing in Premiere Pro, After Effects, or another editor.
